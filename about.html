Our Story

About Bagged Up

A small Kenyan boutique built on one idea: fewer, better bags — honestly listed, fairly priced, and delivered to your door.

Bagged Up started the way most good ideas do — with a personal frustration. Great handbags existed out there, but finding one meant endless scrolling through blurry photos, vague descriptions and sellers who disappeared after payment.

So we flipped the model. Instead of listing everything, we list almost nothing. Every piece in the shop was picked by hand, inspected in person, and photographed as-is. What you see is the bag you get.

How we work

  • A tight edit. Dozens of styles, not thousands. If a bag doesn't earn its place, it doesn't make the site.
  • Honest listings. Real photos, real condition notes, real prices. No filters doing heavy lifting.
  • Zero-risk ordering. Check your bag in front of the delivery rider. Pay by M-Pesa or cash only once it's in your hands.
  • Humans, not bots. Every inquiry is answered by a person who can tell you exactly how the bag looks and feels.

Where we deliver

Anywhere in Kenya. Nairobi orders arrive same-day or next-day by rider; everywhere else arrives in 1–3 days through trusted courier partners. Delivery is free on orders over KSh 5,000.

One promise: if a bag ever arrives not-as-described, tell us within 48 hours and we'll exchange it or refund you. Simple.
