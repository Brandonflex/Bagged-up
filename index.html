Our Story

Chosen slowly, so you can shop quickly

Bagged Up began with a simple frustration: great handbags, buried under infinite scrolling and questionable listings. We do the digging for you — sourcing a small, rotating edit of pieces we'd carry ourselves.

Every bag is inspected in person before it reaches the site. If it isn't something we'd gift our own sisters, it doesn't make the collection.
