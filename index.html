Our Story

Chosen slowly, so you can shop quickly

Bagged Up started with one frustration: good bags were hard to find, and the listings were worse. So we do the digging for you and keep a small, rotating edit of pieces we'd carry ourselves.

Every bag is inspected in person before it reaches the site. If we wouldn't give it to someone we love, it doesn't make the edit.
