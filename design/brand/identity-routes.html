A brand with a point of view

Three ways to make Bagged Up unmistakable.

The current wordmark, type, palette and themes are a first pass, not a locked identity. These are three visual territories for us to compare before changing the live storefront. The sample marks and colors are studies; none is the final logo or a claim about the products.

A contemporary city point of view: tactile stone and clay, dark green ink and a loop motif drawn from bag construction. Locally aware without leaning on decorative clichés.

Mark

Type
Humanist sans for clarity, expressive serif for warmth and contrast.
Images

Trade-off
