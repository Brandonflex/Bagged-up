Frequently asked questions

How do I order?

Add pieces to your bag, then tap “Checkout via WhatsApp”. Your order arrives as a pre-filled message. Send it and we'll confirm availability, delivery time and the total. No account, no online payment.

How do I pay?

On delivery: M-Pesa or cash. You only pay when the bag is physically in your hands. For countrywide courier orders we may request payment of the delivery fee upfront; the bag itself is still paid on delivery.

How long does delivery take?

Nairobi: same-day or next-day via rider (KSh 250). Countrywide: 1–3 days via courier partners (KSh 400). Orders over KSh 5,000 ship free.

Can I see the bag before paying?

Yes, that's the default. The rider waits while you open the package. If it's not what you ordered or arrives damaged, hand it back; you owe nothing.

What if the bag has an issue after delivery?

Tell us within 48 hours with a photo or video. If a piece is defective or not as described we'll exchange it or refund you, including delivery costs on our side.

See the full policy on the Returns & Exchanges page.

Are the designer-named pieces authentic?

We describe every piece exactly as it is. Where a bag is an original designer piece, we say so and stand behind it. Where a piece is an inspired or unbranded style, the listing says so plainly.

If you ever receive something different from its description, that's a returns case, and you're covered.

Do you restock sold-out pieces?

Sometimes. Popular styles do come back. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll tell you if (or when) a piece returns, and suggest close alternatives in stock now.

How do I take care of my bag?

Keep it out of long, direct sunlight; wipe spills with a soft dry cloth; store it stuffed with clean paper in its dust bag. Full care notes are on every product page.

Still stuck? Message us on WhatsApp. We reply within business hours.
